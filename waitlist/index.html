Questions

Does Brandlyft post without me?

Not without your decision. On TikTok you approve every video yourself, as TikTok requires. On Instagram and Facebook you approve every post unless you switch on autopilot yourself. You can switch it off again at any time, for each channel separately.

Does an AI character have to speak for me?

No. A speaking brand character is an option for videos, not a requirement. You decide whether it appears in your content.

Will it still sound like me?

Brandlyft reads your website and suggests a brand profile: products, colours, tone of voice and things you would never say. You confirm or correct it before the first post is created. It shows your real products, not invented ones.

What does Brandlyft cost?

We plan €79, €199 and €449 per month depending on volume and channels, cancel monthly. Early members get special conditions. You will know the exact price before you sign anything.

Where is my data stored?

Our server and storage are in the EU. For text and images we use AI services from Google and Anthropic, details are in the Privacy Policy. You get a data processing agreement before you start.

Does Brandlyft protect me from legal trouble?

Review agents check every post against fixed rules before it is published, for example no health claims, no fake reviews and no false scarcity. This does not replace legal advice. You are responsible for what appears on your channel, which is why you approve it.

When does it start?

We are waiting for Meta and TikTok to approve publishing. After that we start with a small number of businesses and set each one up personally. People on the waitlist hear first.
