How it works

Set up in about ten minutes. After that, Brandlyft creates new content every day. Instagram and Facebook can run on autopilot. On TikTok you review each video and publish it with your own settings.

Step 1

Paste your website

Brandlyft reads your whole site like a browser: products, prices, variants, availability, colours, fonts and tone of voice. Shops are read from the product feed, so facts stay correct.

Step 2

Confirm your brand

You see how Brandlyft understood your brand and correct only what is wrong. Your rules, for example claims you never make, are respected in every post.

Step 3

Connect your channels

Connect Instagram, Facebook and TikTok with the official login of each platform. Choose how much to post. On Instagram and Facebook you approve each post or let Brandlyft publish on autopilot. TikTok videos always wait for your review.

Step 4

Publish and learn

Brandlyft plans, creates and publishes. It measures views, watch time, shares and shop visits, tests new formats and puts more effort into what works.

Built for brands that sell real products

Most AI tools produce generic content. Brandlyft is built around your actual products and checks every result before it goes live.

True to your product

Every image and video is generated from your real product photos and checked against them: colour, shape and details. No invented products.

Quality gate

Independent review agents score every concept and every finished post. Only what passes is published.

Reels with characters

Short videos with a brand character that speaks in your language, lip-synced, with subtitles, in the format each platform favours.

Honest marketing rules

No fake reviews, no invented statistics, no false scarcity. Claims come only from your own product information.

A learning loop you can see

Every month you see what was tested, what worked and what Brandlyft changed as a result.

Made in Germany

Built with European data protection in mind. Data is stored in the EU wherever our providers allow it.

Questions

Does Brandlyft post without my approval?

On Instagram and Facebook only if you choose autopilot, and you can switch at any time, per channel. On TikTok never: you review every video, choose who can see it and publish it yourself, as TikTok requires.

Which platforms are supported?

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok at launch, connected through each platform's official login and API. More platforms will follow.

What happens with my account data?

We only use the permissions you grant to publish your content and read its performance. You can disconnect at any time. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Is AI-generated content labelled?

You decide. Brandlyft supports the labelling options each platform offers.
