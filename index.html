Questions

Good to know.

Does Brandlyft post without my approval?

No. Today Brandlyft only publishes what you submit yourself. An autopilot for Instagram and Facebook is planned: it would be off by default, and only you could switch it on, per channel. On TikTok there is no autopilot: you review every video, choose who can see it and publish it yourself, as TikTok requires.

What happens to my website in the preview?

Brandlyft only reads public pages of your website, like a visitor would, and respects robots.txt. We keep the result for 7 days so the same website is not analysed twice. Nothing gets posted. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Which platforms are supported?

Instagram and Facebook at launch, connected through each platform's official login and API. TikTok is coming soon: the connection is built and waiting for TikTok's approval. More platforms will follow.

What happens with my account data?

We only use the permissions you grant to publish your content and read its performance. Your data and content are used only for your own workspace, never for other customers and never to train AI models. You can disconnect at any time. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Is AI-generated content labelled?

Yes, from launch. Images and videos that Brandlyft creates with AI carry an invisible, machine-readable marking, and Brandlyft sets the AI label on Instagram and TikTok by default. You can switch it off for single pieces that, after your review, do not pass anything off as real, except for brand characters and, once it exists, on autopilot.
