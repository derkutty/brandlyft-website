How it works

Brandlyft is in early access. Reading your website, confirming your brand, connecting your channels and publishing your own videos work today. From the public launch, Brandlyft creates new content every day. You approve each post before it goes live. An autopilot for Instagram and Facebook is planned and would be off by default. On TikTok you review each video and publish it with your own settings.

Step 1

Paste your website

Brandlyft reads your website: products, prices, variants, colours, fonts and tone of voice. Shopify shops are read from the product feed, so facts stay correct.

Step 2

Confirm your brand

You see how Brandlyft understood your brand and correct only what is wrong. Your rules, for example claims you never make, are respected in every post.

Step 3

Connect your channels

Connect Instagram, Facebook and TikTok with the official login of each platform. From launch you choose how much to post and approve each post. TikTok videos always wait for your review.

Step 4

Publish and learn

From launch, Brandlyft plans, creates and publishes. It analyses views, watch time and shares, tests new formats and puts more effort into what works.

Built for brands that sell real products

Most AI tools produce generic content. Brandlyft is built around your actual products. This is how your content is made from the public launch:

True to your product

Every image and video is generated from your real product photos and checked against them: colour, shape and details. No invented products.

Review before every post

Independent review agents score every concept and every finished post and show you what they notice. The decision stays with you.

Reels with characters

Short videos with a brand character that speaks in your language, lip-synced, with subtitles, labelled as AI. From the Growth plan, off by default. Whether you use it is up to you.

Honest marketing rules

No fake reviews, no invented statistics, no false scarcity. Claims come only from your own product information.

A learning loop you can see

From the Growth plan, Brandlyft tests formats and learns from your numbers. On the Pro plan a monthly report shows what was tested, what worked and what Brandlyft changed as a result.

Made in Germany

Built with European data protection in mind. Our server, storage and AI analysis run on Google Cloud in the EU.

Questions

Does Brandlyft post without my approval?

No. Today Brandlyft only publishes what you submit yourself. An autopilot for Instagram and Facebook is planned: it would be off by default, and only you could switch it on, per channel. On TikTok there is no autopilot: you review every video, choose who can see it and publish it yourself, as TikTok requires.

Which platforms are supported?

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok at launch, connected through each platform's official login and API. More platforms will follow.

What happens with my account data?

We only use the permissions you grant to publish your content and read its performance. Your data and content are used only for your own workspace, never for other customers and never to train AI models. You can disconnect at any time. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Is AI-generated content labelled?

Yes, from launch. Images and videos that Brandlyft creates with AI carry an invisible, machine-readable marking, and Brandlyft sets the AI label on Instagram and TikTok by default. You can switch it off for single pieces that, after your review, do not pass anything off as real, except for brand characters and, once it exists, on autopilot.
