Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how Brandlyft (“we”, “us”) processes personal data when you visit this website and when you use the Brandlyft service. A German version is available as Datenschutzerklärung.

1. Controller

Kjell Kuttler, Brandlyft, Richard-Zanders-Straße 53, 51469 Bergisch Gladbach, Germany. E-mail: hello@brandlyft.app.

2. Visiting this website

This website uses no cookies, no tracking and no analytics tools, and loads no fonts or scripts from third parties. The website and the app interface are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA, a subsidiary of Microsoft). When you visit the site, the hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address and the time of the request to deliver the page and protect against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. The page language follows your browser's language setting. If you change it with the DE or EN switch, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage (localStorage). This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

3. Contacting us

If you write to us by e-mail, we process your message and contact details to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them when they are no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply.

4. Waitlist

If you join the waitlist at brandlyft.app/waitlist (in German at brandlyft.app/warteliste), we process your e-mail address and, if provided, your website and industry to tell you when your spot is ready and how Brandlyft is progressing. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We use double opt-in: you receive an e-mail with a confirmation link that is valid for 7 days, and you are only added once you confirm. As proof we store the time and IP address of sign-up and confirmation and the wording of the consent in our storage at Google Cloud in the EU. Unconfirmed entries are deleted after 30 days at the latest. E-mails are sent through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, Paris, France), which also holds the list of confirmed addresses. We keep confirmed entries until you withdraw your consent, at the latest until 12 months after the official launch of Brandlyft. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to hello@brandlyft.app or via the unsubscribe link in our e-mails to the list. We then delete your data, except the proof that you consented and withdrew, which we keep for 3 years.

5. Using the Brandlyft service

When you use the Brandlyft app, we process:

  • Sign-in: you sign in with TikTok or Facebook; there is no password with us. We receive your user ID and your name or display name, for TikTok also your username and profile picture. To keep you signed in, your browser stores a session token in its local storage (localStorage). On our server we only keep a hash of it, valid for 30 days. We currently do not collect an e-mail address, business name or billing information.
  • Brand profile: if you enter the website of your business, our server fetches the home page and a few sub-pages (for example about, team, FAQ, shipping), for Shopify shops also the public product feed. An AI model by Google (Gemini) suggests a brand profile from this content, which you confirm or edit. This content can include names and photos of people, for example on team pages. The analysis runs through Google's EU endpoint, and the data stays in the EU.
  • Videos: videos you upload, with title, caption, duration and publishing history.
  • Connected social media accounts: when you connect Instagram, Facebook or TikTok through the official login of the platform, we receive an access token and the data covered by the permissions you grant (see section 6).

In the app, your browser loads profile and preview images directly from Meta and TikTok, product images from the website of your business, and videos through time-limited links from our storage at Google Cloud (storage.googleapis.com). These providers receive your IP address. Legal basis is the performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Where we process personal data on behalf of our business customers, we act as a processor under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).

6. Data from Meta (Instagram, Facebook) and TikTok

We only request the permissions needed for the features you use and use the data only to provide those features to you:

  • Basic account information (account ID, name, profile picture) to show which account is connected.
  • Facebook Pages and the linked Instagram account, so you can choose where to publish.
  • Publishing permissions to publish the videos you submit in Brandlyft: as Reels on Instagram and Facebook, and on TikTok as a post or as a draft in your TikTok account. We only publish when you submit a post in the app.
  • Insights and video statistics (for example views, reach, likes, comments, shares) of your recent posts, which we show to you but do not store.

We do not sell this data, do not use it for advertising profiles and do not share it with third parties except the service providers listed in section 7. Access tokens are kept in our storage at Google Cloud in the EU, which Google encrypts by default. The app in your browser never receives them. If you disconnect an account in Brandlyft, we revoke the token with the platform, delete it and stop processing data of that account. Data from TikTok is used in accordance with the TikTok Developer Terms of Service, data from Meta in accordance with the Meta Platform Terms.

7. Service providers

  • Google Cloud (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC): server (Cloud Run in Frankfurt), storage (Cloud Storage in the EU), server logs, and the AI model Gemini for the brand profile through the EU endpoint.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosting of this website and the app interface, and automated processes.
  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. and TikTok Technology Ltd. (Ireland): sign-in, publishing and statistics through their official APIs.
  • STRATO AG (Germany): domain and e-mail.
  • Sendinblue SAS (Brevo) (France): sending waitlist e-mails.

Transfers to countries outside the EU are based on adequacy decisions (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.

8. Retention

We keep your account, connections, brand profile and videos until you delete them or your workspace (section 10). When the cooperation with your business ends, we delete this data within 30 days, unless statutory retention obligations (for example for invoices) apply. Server logs are deleted after 30 days.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR), and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the data protection authority of North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW). To exercise your rights, write to hello@brandlyft.app.

10. Data deletion requests

You can delete the data Brandlyft holds about you or your connected accounts in three ways:

  1. In the app: under “Your data”, “Delete my Brandlyft data” deletes your workspace immediately after a confirmation: your account, the connections to Meta and TikTok, the brand profile and all stored videos. We revoke the access tokens with the platforms first. This also signs you out on all devices.
  2. By e-mail: send an e-mail with the subject “Delete my data” to hello@brandlyft.app and name the connected account (platform and username). We delete your data and confirm the deletion within 30 days.
  3. Through Facebook, Instagram or TikTok: if you remove Brandlyft in the settings of your account under connected apps, our access ends immediately. We delete the data stored about that account as soon as you write to us or delete your workspace in the app.

Copies in server logs and deleted files that our storage keeps briefly for recovery are removed after 30 days at the latest. Posts you have already published stay on the platform, where you can delete them in your account.
