2. The service

Brandlyft is an online service for businesses. Today Brandlyft reads the website of a business and drafts a brand profile with the help of AI, which the customer reviews, corrects and confirms. It keeps a library of videos that the customer uploads or that Brandlyft prepares for the customer under an individual agreement. It publishes a video on connected accounts on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok only when the customer submits it, and it shows statistics of recent posts. Further functions, for example creating content with AI, scheduling and reports, are planned. Brandlyft only owes them once it has made them available and described them in a service description or an individual offer.

3. Account and connected platforms

4. Responsibility for content

The customer remains responsible for the content published on its accounts, in particular for the accuracy of product information, compliance with advertising law and the labelling of AI-generated content. Brandlyft prepares content based on the information provided by the customer and its website and does not knowingly invent reviews, statistics or claims.
